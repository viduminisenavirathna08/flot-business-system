Add Barcodes (Post Office / Courier)

Paste the barcodes you got (one per line, or a range like FL000001-FL000100). They are kept as Available Barcodes. When a parcel is Confirmed it automatically gets the next available barcode of its delivery type (Post Office / Speed Post / Courier). Parcels already waiting get barcodes right away, oldest first. To give a barcode to a specific parcel write its phone after it: FL000005 0763058296. Barcodes can be changed in the table below.

Confirmed Parcels

Type the barcode (tracking number) of each parcel and Save. Returned parcels are counted as a loss in the Budget (no income; packing, delivery and ad cost remain). "Track" copies the barcode and opens SL Post / Domex.

Item Costs (by Item Code)

Costs are kept in itemcost.xlsx (project folder). Each Code can have a row per Quantity (1, 2, ...) with the full cost for that quantity. If a quantity has no row, the Quantity 1 cost x quantity is used. Packing Cost blank = default. Edit here and Save, or upload an Excel with the columns: Code, Name, Category, Quantity, Item cost, Packing cost.
